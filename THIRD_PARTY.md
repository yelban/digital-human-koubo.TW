Services you connect with your own account / 需要你自己账号的服务:
- [grok CLI](https://docs.x.ai) by xAI — video generation with `reference_to_video` (Grok Imagine Video 1.5) and scene images with `image_edit`. Generation uses the weekly allowance of your grok account. The video tools work only when zero data retention (ZDR) is off in `/privacy`.
- [Xiaomi MiMo](https://platform.xiaomimimo.com/) — optional TTS (voice design / voice cloning). Clone only voices you own or are licensed to use.

Research reports in `skills/digital-human-koubo/references/research/` describe editing techniques observed in publicly available videos, with timestamps for reference. They contain no copied footage, and quotes are kept under 15 words.
